# Roadmap

## Live

- Search films and shows
- Title page: poster, facts, UK certificate, IMDb / Rotten Tomatoes / Metacritic scores, trailer
- Shared watchlist (add from search or a title), filter by films / TV / on our services
- Seen: films marked seen, and shows ticked up to the latest episode (back on the watchlist when a new one airs)
- Your services; where to watch on each title, yours first
- Episode tracking (shared): tick episodes or whole seasons; Up next on the watchlist and each show
- Push alerts, each title checked once a day: new episodes of shows you're caught up on, new seasons of ones on the list, and watchlist titles arriving on your services

## Later

- Calendar of upcoming episodes, and a feed for Google Calendar
- Trending, recommendations, cast pages
- "Leaving soon" and deep links into the streaming apps (Watchmode)
- Your own ratings and notes; imports from Netflix and Trakt; stats
- "What shall we watch tonight?" picker
